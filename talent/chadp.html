Professional Introduction

Chad is a powerhouse event professional and Event Manager with extensive leadership experience across every facet of professional staffing. Relocating to Las Vegas from Los Angeles, Chad previously built a high-profile career as a reality television casting producer and general manager of five elite Hollywood nightclubs.

He combines entertainment production precision with executive hospitality leadership, executing major activations for world-class brands like Nike, Google, Gatorade, Dell, Coca-Cola, Amazon, and Formula 1. Whether leading high-volume lead generation, directing production teams, or managing VIP convention registration, Chad consistently brings executive-level coordination and exceptional energy to every event floor.

Skills & Specialized Experience

Event Management & Production: Proven expertise as an Event Manager, Sourcing Manager, and Production Assistant for tier-1 brand activations, sports leagues, and televised broadcasts.

Lead Generation & Sales: Former Head Lead Generator for Las Vegas platforms and specialized sales leads for automotive and technology expos (SEMA / Vantrue Dashcams).

High-Profile Hospitality & VIP: Decades of venue management experience, VIP registration lead, and hospitality activation lead for luxury brands (Jack Daniels F1, Hermès, Pfizer).

Versatile On-Site Roles: Experienced across product specialty, ticket operations, costume character mascot performance, and booth logistics.

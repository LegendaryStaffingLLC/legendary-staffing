Professional Introduction

Michelle is an elite, highly experienced National Tour Manager, Automotive Specialist, and Event Director with over 15 years of industry excellence. Articulate, tech-savvy, and adaptable, she possesses a proven track record of directing massive teams (managing 80+ Brand Ambassadors at scale) and overseeing high-stakes activations for global brands.

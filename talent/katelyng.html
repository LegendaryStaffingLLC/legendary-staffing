Professional Introduction

Katelyn is a versatile, high-impact Automotive Product Specialist, Team Lead, and Brand Ambassador with over 6 years of industry experience across major conventions, trade shows, sporting events, and automotive tours. As a native bilingual speaker in both English and Spanish, she excels at establishing immediate rapport, conducting one-on-one consumer engagements, and elevating brand presence.

Her background features key representations for global powerhouses including Stellantis (Dodge, RAM, Jeep, Chrysler), Amazon, and TikTok. From managing thrill-ride operations and vehicle walk-arounds at Barrett-Jackson and Mecum Auctions to coordinating executive keynotes and VIP hospitality at AWS and CES, Katelyn pairs polished presentation with seamless operational execution.

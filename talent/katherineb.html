Professional Introduction

Katherine is a dynamic, outgoing Bilingual Brand Ambassador with 5 years of hands-on experience in experiential marketing, high-traffic conventions, trade shows, and music festivals. Fully fluent in English and Spanish, she brings positive energy, sharp adaptability, and a highly engaging demeanor to every brand she represents.

Her diverse portfolio spans major global tech, sports, and consumer brands—including Nike, Google, ESPN, Coca-Cola, and Dell Technologies—as well as premier spirits activations for brands like Tito's and Casamigos. Whether navigating high-profile awards shows like the Latin Grammys or massive international events like Formula 1 and EDC, Katherine consistently delivers exceptional customer experiences.
